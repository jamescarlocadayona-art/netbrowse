Security Advisor

Your safety is our top priority. PINCH NetBrowse is built with multiple layers of defense.

Built-in Protection

  • HTTPS Validation: We never bypass or ignore invalid SSL certificates.
  • Download Scanning: Dangerous extensions are flagged, and SHA-256 hashes are calculated.
  • AdBlocker & Anti-Tracking: Block malicious ads and third-party trackers automatically.
  • Profile Isolation: Cookies, cache, and history are strictly separated between profiles.
  • Incognito Mode: Leave no trace of your browsing history on the local machine.

If you encounter a security warning, it is highly recommended that you heed it. PINCH NetBrowse will never download or execute files without your explicit consent.

Support & FAQs

Need help? We've got you covered.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why does the browser look old?

A: PINCH NetBrowse was intentionally designed to replicate the classic Windows 98/2000 look while running on top of the modern Qt WebEngine.


Q: How do I block ads on a specific site?

A: Click the Shield icon in the toolbar, or go to Settings -> AdBlocker and add the domain to the allowlist.


Q: Where are my downloads saved?

A: By default, they go to your system's Downloads folder. You can change this in Settings -> Downloads.


Q: The browser is slow, how can I fix it?

A: Ensure you have hardware acceleration enabled. If you still have issues, you may need to clear the cache via Tools -> Clear Cache.

About PINCH NetBrowse

The story behind the browser.

PINCH NetBrowse is a labor of love, created for the PINCH Neptune operating system. It bridges the gap between the nostalgic user interfaces of the late 1990s and the powerful, modern web standards of today.


Built entirely in Python using PySide6 and Qt WebEngine, PINCH NetBrowse proves that you don't need to sacrifice functionality for aesthetics. Whether you are researching a paper with our Citation tool, blocking trackers with our AdBlocker, or just browsing your favorite channels, PINCH NetBrowse delivers a uniquely classic experience.
