Features & Web Accessories

Discover the powerful features implemented in the current version of PINCH Neptune.

Core Browsing

  • Modern Engine: Powered by Microsoft WebView2 (Chromium).

Security Advisor

Your safety is our top priority. PINCH Neptune is built with multiple layers of defense.

Built-in Protection

  • HTTPS Validation: We never bypass or ignore invalid SSL certificates.
  • AdBlocker & Anti-Tracking: Block malicious ads and third-party trackers automatically.
  • Unsafe Site Blocking: Known phishing and malware domains are intercepted before they load.
  • Profile Isolation: InPrivate browsing sessions are strictly separated and wiped clean upon closure.
  • Privacy Settings: Do Not Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) headers are sent.

If you encounter a security warning, it is highly recommended that you heed it. PINCH Neptune will never download or execute files without your explicit consent.

Support & FAQs

Need help? We've got you covered.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why does the browser look old?

A: PINCH Neptune was intentionally designed to replicate the classic Windows Neptune look while running on top of the modern Microsoft WebView2 engine. It is built using C# Windows Forms.


Q: How do I block ads on a specific site?

A: Click the Shield icon in the toolbar to open the Security Center, or go to Tools -> Internet Options -> Privacy and add the domain to the allowlist.


Q: Where are my downloads saved?

A: By default, they go to your system's Downloads folder. You can change this in Tools -> Internet Options -> General.


Q: How do I use InPrivate mode?

A: Press Ctrl+Shift+N or go to File -> New InPrivate Tab to open a fully isolated private window.

About PINCH Neptune Browser

The story behind the browser.

PINCH Neptune Browser is a labor of love, created for the modern web. It bridges the gap between the nostalgic user interfaces of the late 1990s and the powerful, modern web standards of today.


Built entirely in C# using Windows Forms and Microsoft WebView2, PINCH Neptune proves that you don't need to sacrifice functionality for aesthetics. Whether you are researching a paper with our Citation tool, blocking trackers with our AdBlocker, or just browsing your favorite channels, PINCH Neptune delivers a uniquely classic experience.
